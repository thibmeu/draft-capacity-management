



Web Bot Auth                                                  T. Meunier
Internet-Draft                                                Cloudflare
Intended status: Informational                           2 November 2026
Expires: 6 May 2027


                     Bot rate limit is all you need
                 draft-webbotauth-meunier-brian-latest

Abstract

   This document describes a profile of [MOLE-ARCH] that allows for rate
   limit of clients.  The goal is to allow clients to provide
   information that sites can use to apply rate limit policies without
   requiring a stable identifier.
